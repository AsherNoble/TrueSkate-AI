# M1-MULTITAP91-20260925 — Full-screen 7 × 13 tap grid

Status: three bounded XR2 recordings saved; full grid **not** visually reproduced.
This is a diagnostic, not a training-data collection run or adopted calibration.

Following the visible [four-touch test](M1-MULTITAP4-20260925.md), the operator
requested a maximum-area grid. The XR2 control profile's 16-point expanded
hitboxes leave a largest integer-centre rectangle of approximately
`x=92..413`, `y=144..790`. The probe inset its 7 columns to `x=100..400` and
13 rows to `y=160..760`, with exactly 50 logical points between neighbours:
91 requested positions. Every point passes the protected-control check. There
is no single-touch control in the recordings; a reset occurs before recording.
The offline payload test confirms 91 distinct pointer sources with all downs
on the same W3C action tick.

One unrecorded capability check and three recorded commands all returned
success, and post-command screenshots stayed in gameplay. The three command
round trips took 7.94, 7.56, and 30.51 s; their originals decoded to 392,
380, and 1,069 frames, all within the one-minute recorder cap.

However, frame inspection shows only a handful of bright spots, concentrated
near the rightmost requested column. An exploratory brightness check across
the entire recordings found **five** requested positions brightening in each
recording, and no comparable brightening at the other 86 positions. Its method
and detected positions are in
[visual-check.json](../evidence/M1-MULTITAP91-20260925/visual-check.json).
This establishes that Appium/WDA command success does **not** imply that all
91 touches are visibly delivered. Whether the loss occurs inside XCTest,
iOS touch handling, or True Skate is unresolved; a specific five-touch hardware
limit has not been established by this experiment.
