# M1-SETTLE-20260925 — Wait for a still scene before the start control

Status: bounded smoke evidence on both XRs in SLS 2015 Los Angeles. No
training data was collected: all runs used `--die-five-experiment`, which
retains originals and never spawns the aligner. **The flag is not enabled in
any production launcher.** Enabling it is an operator decision.

## Hypothesis

False early start-control detections (M1-AUDIT, M1-ONSET-VALIDATION) happen
because the board and camera are still moving under the screen centre when
the start control fires after the pre-segment reset (M1-DIE5-COMPARE notes).
Waiting until the centre is still should remove the condition.

## Method

`--start-settle-threshold T --start-settle-max-s M` (commit `7b57d4f`) runs
after the existing reset and 1.5 s sleep. It polls Appium screenshots and
compares the ±90 pt centre box, decoded at 256 px width. It continues once two
successive mean absolute grey differences are below `T`, or once `M` seconds
pass. The result is stored in the segment manifest as `start_settle`.

`T = 1.5` came from Phase 2 footage. Using 0.25 s-spaced frames before the start
control, still boards measured ≤0.58 and moving boards ≥2.9.

Rig code `8a1ff6f`, staged under
`/Users/training-server/trueskate-ai-runtime/tmp/die5-compare-phase2-20260925/code-settle-8a1ff6f`,
ran with release `1b8497e`'s venv. Seeds were 2509252001–3, 2509253001–4 and
2509254001–4.

## Results

[Manifests](../evidence/M1-SETTLE-20260925/settle-manifests.json),
[motion](../evidence/M1-SETTLE-20260925/settle15-motion.json) and
[A/B analysis](../evidence/M1-SETTLE-20260925/settle15-analysis.json):

| Run | Settled | Settle time | Pre-start centre motion | Start A = B | A rate range |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| XR2, max 6 s | 0/3 | timed out (6.0–6.7 s) | 0.059–0.073 (still) | 3/3 | −175 to +284 ppm |
| XR1, max 15 s | 4/4 | 7.36, 7.38, 7.54, 1.81 s | 0.044–0.063 (still) | 4/4 | −166 to +787 ppm |
| XR2, max 15 s | 4/4 | 7.41, 7.39, 2.17, 7.23 s | 0.053–0.059 (still) | 4/4 | −178 to +46 ppm |

- **Motion curve.** Screenshot differences follow the same transient each
  time. After the 1.5 s reset sleep they rise from ~6 to a peak of ~50, then
  decay to <0.4 about 7–8 s after the reset. The two ~2 s cases were already
  still.
- **Comparison.** Same devices and park earlier the same day, without the
  wait: 9/30 recordings had a moving board at the start control, and 1/30
  produced an A start anchor anomaly (+6,056 ppm).
- **With the wait:** 11/11 recordings were still at the start control, with
  0/11 anomalies and A equal to B on every start.
- **Cost.** About 6 s per segment, roughly 10% of one-minute segment
  throughput.
- **Limits.** Small n, one park, one session. The ~6 s saturation-free window
  after `M=6` also removed motion in the XR2 runs without ever reporting
  "settled". The benefit is the extra delay itself, and the settle report
  makes it adaptive and auditable.

One XR1 run had a die-five end-marker rejection. That weakness of the
preregistered five-touch detector B follows swipes, not resets, and the wait
does not address it.

## Recommendation for the operator

If Los Angeles collection resumes, add `--start-settle-threshold 1.5
--start-settle-max-s 15` to the linear launcher. Consider skipping, rather than
recording, a segment that fails to settle; this is not implemented yet
(currently it records and logs `settled: false`). The end anchor is not
covered. A larger confirmation across parks should precede making the flag a
default.

## End-control settle and settle-required skip (commit `ead2d97`)

`--end-settle-threshold T --end-settle-max-s M` waits for a still centre
before the end control. The wait comes out of the existing 7 s end reserve,
and M must leave at least 2 s of it. `--start-settle-required` skips an
unsettled segment without recording; three skips in a row exit with the
recovery code.

- **Baseline, no end wait (Phase 2, 30 recordings):** 7/30 end controls had a
  moving centre (motion >1). Both end-anchor problems in Phase 2 fell in that
  group: one A/B one-frame disagreement and one B no-result
  ([phase2-end-motion](../evidence/M1-SETTLE-20260925/phase2-end-motion.json)).
- **Bounded run, both waits (3 XR1 + 3 XR2, seeds 2509255011–3/21–3):**
  - All 6 start and all 6 end waits settled. Start waits took 1.9–7.6 s.
    End waits took 1.6–2.0 s, which is the two-poll minimum, so the ends were
    already still.
  - Pre-start motion was ≤0.061 and pre-end motion ≤0.022 in all 6.
  - A = B on all 12 anchors, with no B no-results. Rates were −32 to +604 ppm.
  - Evidence: [start](../evidence/M1-SETTLE-20260925/endsettle-start-motion.json),
    [end](../evidence/M1-SETTLE-20260925/endsettle-end-motion.json) and
    [analysis](../evidence/M1-SETTLE-20260925/endsettle-analysis.json).

Across both settle runs, 17/17 settle-condition recordings had still anchors,
with no rate beyond ±800 ppm. Without the wait, 1/30 same-day recordings
exceeded 0.002 and 9/30 starts were moving. n is small and covers one park.
