# M1-20260719 — Full ungating of heatmap touch supervision

- Source: July 19 entries in archived `experiments/vision_sequence_leap_journal.md`
  (ARCH-001). Historical result, not rerun during cleanup.
- Question: does supervising command-labelled touches even when their rendered
  trace is absent recover the per-frame heatmap model's missing recall?
- Change: `require_trace=False` with the existing corpus and otherwise matched
  training configuration. Positive supervision increased about 3.4×.
- Recorded result: precision fell to about 3.8%; negative specificity fell
  from roughly 90% to 58%. By epoch 6, approximately 189 absolute touches matched,
  compared with approximately 184 in the previous arm, alongside many more
  spurious detections. The run was stopped early.
- Interpretation: hard ungating did not solve localisation for this model and
  corpus. Percent recall denominators changed, so compare absolute matches too.
  This is not evidence that the newer clip-level linear regressor has the same ceiling.

  checkpoint path are not established by the journal excerpt. The full original
  record and neighbouring tuning/stroke-recovery experiments remain in ARCH-001.
