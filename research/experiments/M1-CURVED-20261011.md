## Implementation

commands, feasible random/arc sampling, persistent global attempt accounting,
five-round qualification, minute native-60-Hz capture, random consecutive
120-frame windows, a native-frame audit viewer, balanced whole-session selection,
dense path learning, constrained decoding, resumable training, one-time test
exposure and qualitative human overlays. Existing linear implementations and
checkpoint formats are unchanged.

The complete 24-park operator library is preserved verbatim in `config/parks.json`.
The reproducible five-park draw is Inbound, Newark, 2015 Super Crown, 2015
LosAngeles and Macba, using their exact names in all data. Munich remains reserved.

The separate Modal wrapper prepares byte-preserving sequential bundles and quotes
cost offline. Upload and compute require explicit approved budgets; no paid work
was launched during implementation.
