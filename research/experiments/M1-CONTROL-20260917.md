# M1-CONTROL-20260917 — XR2 control-start contamination test

Status: failed on device after 2/300 gestures; evidence preserved.

## Question

Does the versioned control-start exclusion map prevent accidental UI/control
activation while preserving linear paths that cross or end inside controls?

## Fixed protocol

- Device: XR2 only; existing instrumented WDA, Appium and recording tunnel.
- Exactly 300 separate constant-speed linear swipes, seed 20260917.
- All 13 mapped controls receive balanced coverage (23 or 24 swipes each).
- Every touch-down is outside the control's 16-logical-point safety region.
  Every path crosses and ends inside its assigned effective hitbox.
- Durations cycle through 0.25, 0.35 and 0.50 seconds.
- At most 25 swipes per recording; capture is capped at 55 seconds and never
  retried after a recording-start failure.
- Reset occurs before recording, followed by a 1.5-second settling interval.
  No reset occurs inside a recording.
- Each recording has separate 50 ms centre-screen start/end calibration holds.
- Save pre/post screenshots for every swipe and check foreground, menu, editor
  and Bolt-modal state after every swipe. Preserve all videos for visual review
  of reset, rewind, camera, fast-forward and spin effects that lack a complete
  automatic state detector.
- Output is isolated and permanently marked diagnostic-only; no clip may enter
  a training corpus.

## Acceptance rule

Pass only if all 300 swipes execute, every WDA timing report is complete, every
recording remains under one minute, automatic checks find zero contamination,
and visual review finds zero accidental control activations. Preserve a failure
without replacement or exclusion.

## Result

Run 01 stopped at its first automatic contamination finding, as specified. The
first swipe, aimed from `(0.498643, 0.867611)` into the transient Community cell,
did not activate it. The second swipe began at `(0.723953, 0.142000)` and moved
upward into the Camera region, ending at `(0.723953, 0.064161)` over 350 ms. It
opened the Replay editor. Pre/post screenshots and the recording confirm the
state change; the WDA report contains all three submitted actions (start control
plus two swipes), with no dropped records. Capture time was 11.20 seconds.

The Camera touch-down was outside the configured 16-point exclusion: about
20.0 logical points below the mapped effective edge and 4.0 points beyond the
expanded boundary. Therefore the current start-only rule is not safe for a path
that immediately heads into this control. This run cannot distinguish an
underestimated physical hitbox from the finger entering the control before True
Skate classifies the contact as a drag. A targeted approach-distance test is the
next useful experiment.

Acceptance failed. The remaining 298 planned swipes were not executed or
replaced.
