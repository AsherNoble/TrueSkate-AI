## Calibration-fit evidence

The six critical Los Angeles segments have two-anchor fit rates
`1.004647–1.012607`; their start-control detector scores are `12.58–15.48`,
while end-control scores are `26.92–36.23`. A false early start-control detection
would raise the fitted rate and place earlier gesture clips before the real
trace. Taking rate `1.0` as an approximate true clock rate, the fit anomaly
predicts the six observed late onsets to within 0.69 displayed-frame slots on
average (individual estimates 3.25–8.17 slots; human observations 3–7).
Two clips from the same Los Angeles segment illustrate the taper: sample 000
was critical near the segment start; sample 016 was only mild later in that
segment. This is strong evidence for a bad anchor rather than a fixed offset
in the compact-video extractor, but the original segment `.mov` was not retained
for direct inspection.

The Super Crown critical clip has the opposite rate anomaly (`0.993938`) and a
lower end-control score (`22.84`); an early end-control detection is compatible
with its two-slot late onset. That mechanism is less certain because only one
such clip was human-labelled critical.

Across all 1,439 aligned segments (13,902 clips), 1,063 clips belong to segments
with `abs(rate - 1.0) > 0.002`: 936 Los Angeles, 47 Skateboard GB, 39 Kansas City,
41 Super Crown, and none Workshop. This is a *screening count*, not a count of
proven bad clips. Most segment fits are tightly near `1.0`; the current fit
accepts the much wider range `0.98–1.02`. The Los Angeles outliers therefore
merit investigation before freezing a training manifest.

## Decision

Do not treat strict-loader admission alone as timing certification, and do not
train on the unfiltered replacement pool yet. First validate an independent
check for both calibration anchors against a small, human-reviewed selection of
outlier and ordinary-rate segments. Then decide whether to exclude, recover or
recollect affected segments while preserving the intended park mixture. No
automatic rate threshold is approved by this audit alone.
