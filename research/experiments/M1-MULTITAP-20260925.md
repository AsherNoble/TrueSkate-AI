# M1-MULTITAP-20260925 — Two-finger visibility probe

Status: operator confirmed two simultaneous visible taps in all three corrected recordings.
This is a diagnostic, not calibration adoption or training data.

## Question and method

Can one WDA action send two simultaneous stationary touches into True Skate
without entering its camera/editor UI, and do two distinct visible marks result?

The operator confirmed XR2 was in SLS 2015 Los Angeles. After a pre-recording
reset and 1.5 s settle, each recording contained an 80 ms one-finger control at
normalised `(0.50, 0.50)`, then an attempted pair at `(0.46, 0.50)` and
`(0.54, 0.50)`. Separate XCTest originals were recorded on XR2; no samples
were admitted to any corpus. Gameplay/editor screenshots were checked around
each attempt.

The first exploratory pass missed the single control because recorder startup
lagged `start()`. Increasing the lead-in to 2.5 s put both commands on film in
the next three recordings. The operator correctly observed no dispersed pair.
Inspection of the installed Selenium implementation showed why:
`ActionChains(driver, devices=fingers)` retained only the **last** `PointerInput`.
An offline capture of its encoded W3C payload contained one source, at
`(0.54, 0.50)`. These first six recordings cannot answer the two-finger
question, despite successful command responses and normal gameplay.

The probe now uses the repository's `perform_pointer_actions` helper. Its
encoded payload was checked before device use: it has two distinct pointer
sources, each with `pointerDown` at the same action index. The exact bounded
three-repetition XR2 run was repeated with this correction. A regression test
checks the two-source payload; the probe is not used for training collection.

## Observations

All three genuine two-pointer WDA requests completed without an exception.
Their originals contain 252, 249 and 274 decoded frames. All three
post-command screenshots passed the gameplay guard; none matched the editor
or menu detector. The operator reviewed the corrected viewer and reported
clearly seeing two simultaneous taps in every repetition. My earlier quick
centre crop missed them. This supports visible two-touch execution, while
calibration timing and automatic detection still require separate validation.
