# M1-MULTITAP-20260925 — Two-finger visibility probe

Status: three corrected XR2 repetitions captured; operator frame review pending.
This is a diagnostic, not calibration adoption or training data.

## Question and method

Can one WDA action send two simultaneous stationary touches into True Skate
without entering its camera/editor UI, and do two distinct visible marks result?

The operator confirmed XR2 was in SLS 2015 Los Angeles. After a pre-recording
reset and 1.5 s settle, each recording contained an 80 ms one-finger control at
normalised `(0.50, 0.50)`, then one W3C Actions command with two 80 ms fingers
at `(0.46, 0.50)` and `(0.54, 0.50)`. The two fingers have equal-length pointer
chains with `pointerDown` on the same action tick. Separate XCTest originals
were recorded on XR2; no samples were admitted to any corpus. Gameplay/editor
screenshots were checked around each attempt.

An exploratory first pass produced three originals, but the single control
began before the video's reported first frame because recording initialisation
lagged the `start()` call. Those originals are retained separately. Increasing
the post-start lead-in from 1.0 to 2.5 s put both commands in all three
corrected recordings. The web viewer presents only the corrected set.

## Observations

All three corrected WDA two-finger requests completed without an exception.
Each original decoded to 245–253 frames. All three post-command screenshots
passed the existing gameplay guard; none matched the editor or menu detector.
This establishes transport and a normal-gameplay outcome for these attempts.
It does **not** yet establish that True Skate rendered two distinct touches.
A quick centre crop was visually ambiguous against the skateboard graphics;
the operator should judge the original videos frame by frame before a larger
multi-point test.
