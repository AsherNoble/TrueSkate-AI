# Trail as a timing instrument — resumed 2026-10-13

**The trail is useful on clear, moving, non-retracing stretches, but these clips do not establish one-native-frame accuracy. Small lead/lag signs depend on cap geometry. Clip 67's purported ≈170 ms executor lead is confounded by retracing, endpoint scoring and deck interference.** No device, rig or paid service was used; `lag/`, clips and repository were read-only.

**Timing limits.** The old edge score transitions around excess 25; its `R_CAP=24` is several pixels smaller than the held-cap edge. Radius error ΔR causes approximately **−ΔR / speed** apparent lag: 4 px means 10 ms at 0.4 px/ms, 4 ms at 1 px/ms, 2 ms at 2 px/ms. An illustrative ±6 px geometry uncertainty alone gives ±15 ms at 0.4 px/ms, before 16.7 ms frame sampling, calibration and rendering uncertainty. Existing ±8 ms direction categories are therefore not robust. Kinks are blurred by the 20–30 px glow kernel; these videos cannot separate subtle executor corner cutting from rendering without independently logged touches.

**Clip 67:** its final moving leg reverses along the preceding leg (direction cosine −0.999968, endpoint cross-line distance 2.56 px). The eventual endpoint was already crossed around command 680 ms. Frames 77–80, observed at 711–761 ms, thus contain recent old glow there, but Viterbi assigns tau 921–933. Its overlap check then incorrectly treats that glow as too old because it uses the ahead candidate time. `past_end` additionally supplies artificial dark/visible evidence beyond the path. The actual head is deck-contaminated. This is a tracker failure mode, not evidence that a 172 ms hold was absorbed.

**Recommended changes (proposals only):** calibrate a cap/threshold interval rather than replace 24 with one asserted truth; model age-dependent width; use at least 250–300 ms as a conservative retrace exclusion window, including candidate-time uncertainty; censor endpoints, holds and spatially repeated locations; never treat space beyond a finite path as observed darkness. Use pre-onset scenery identity rather than the 500 ms persistence rule, which erases genuine long holds. The legacy board proxy flags 26 midpoint crops versus 9 true deck intersections; it also detects rails/text and must not be used as a physical occlusion count. One-frame relative timing is plausible only with strong clear motion, calibrated cap shape, no corners/retraces/holds and independent onset validation; it is not certified here.
